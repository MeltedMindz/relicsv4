# Brief — MEMBRANE

Soft biological tissue seen through a microscope. Cell walls, irregular lobes, a wet organic
edge. Nothing should read as machined or measured: no straight lines, no repeating grid, no
symmetry you could name. The silhouettes should be irregular and different on every token.

Palette: stained-slide pinks and violets, haematoxylin blue, faint cytoplasm grey.

Stress should make the tissue look inflamed and swollen; recovery should let it settle.
