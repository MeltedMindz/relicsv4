# Brief — CAIRN

Stone markers. Monumental and sparse: each token is ONE stacked cairn standing alone in an
empty field, weathered and heavy. Not a pattern, not an ornament, not a mandala — a single
object with a top and a bottom, made of a few large rough courses.

Palette: mineral and muted. Granite grey, lichen green-grey, iron oxide, bone. Nothing bright,
nothing decorative.

Under drawdown the stack should lose courses and read as precarious. On recovery it should
rebuild and settle.
