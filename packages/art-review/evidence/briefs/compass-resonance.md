# Brief — RESONANCE

Nested geometric figures. Each token is one ornamental figure built by repeating a rule on itself,
level on level, so the whole thing is self-similar: what you see at the outside is what you see at
the centre, smaller. Radial and centred is right — this is a rosette, an emblem, a stamped seal.

Every token should be a genuinely different FIGURE, not the same figure resized: some hexagonal,
some circular, some diamond, and the branching should differ too.

Palette: deep indigo ground with two metals — a warm copper and a cold pewter — plus one accent
that appears rarely. Colour should step outward level by level so depth reads as colour.

Recovery should open the figure out: the levels grow toward each other and the figure fills.
Drawdown should cut generations away so fewer levels are drawn and the figure thins.
