# Brief — SIGNAL

Broadcast interference on a dead channel. Dense horizontal scan-lines, dropout, the ghost of a
raster. It should look like a screen carrying no picture, and it should be BUSY — the density is
the subject.

Palette: phosphor green and warm white on near-black, with a thin magenta chroma error.

Volatility should tear the raster apart. Recovery should let the lines re-lock.
