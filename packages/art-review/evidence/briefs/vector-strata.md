# Brief — STRATA

A drill core laid horizontally. Bands of sediment stacked one on another, each a different
thickness, read from the top down as a record of time. Horizontal composition throughout, wide
silhouette, no radial symmetry and no centred emblem.

Palette: cold — glacial blue, slate, wet shale, a thin seam of pale ash.

Drawdown should lay down more beds. Recovery should rule bright lines through the whole field.
