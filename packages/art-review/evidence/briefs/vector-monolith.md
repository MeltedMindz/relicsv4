# Brief — MONOLITH

One slab. A single monumental upright form on an empty ground, and nothing else in the frame.
Extreme sparsity: if a token has more than a handful of marks in it, it is wrong. The whole
weight of the image is one object and the space around it.

Palette: two tones only — a dark ground and one pale stone.

Drawdown should crack the slab. Recovery should heal it.
